Hi, I’m Yanzhe Ji

AI that reasons
and acts.

I’m an undergraduate at the Australian National University and Shandong University. I’m interested in how AI agents use evidence to make decisions and how those decisions translate into action in the physical world.

Explore my papers ↓ OpenReview ↗

Research interests

Multi-agent systems

How can specialized agents share evidence and coordinate on tasks that require reasoning across several steps?

Embodied AI

How can vision-language-action models decide when to act, when to replan, and when to spend more computation?

Selected papers

  1. AACL–IJCNLP 2026 Main conference · Long paper

    SurgAgents: A Multi-agent Framework for Embodied Cataract Surgical Intent Prediction

    Shengyuan Wang*, Yanzhe Ji*, Yue Yao, Zhenyue Qin, Yang Liu

    * Equal contribution

    Specialized agents summarize surgical history into a short narrative. A vision-language model uses that history and the current frame to forecast the surgical phase five seconds ahead, with retrieval for low-confidence cases.

    ACL Anthology ↗ PDF ↗
  2. NeurIPS 2026 Poster

    Reading Attribution from Attention: Evidence Heads as Latent Attribution Mechanisms in LLMs

    Qi Wu, Jianfeng Qu, Yanzhe Ji, Peng-Fei Zhang, Siyu Li, Wei Chen, Zhixu Li, Jiajie Xu

    Identifies attention heads that respond to supporting evidence and uses them to trace individual sentences in a model’s answer back to their sources without additional training.

    OpenReview ↗ PDF ↗
  3. arXiv · 2026 Preprint

    Mind the Rarities: Can Rare Skin Diseases Be Reliably Diagnosed via Diagnostic Reasoning?

    Yang Liu*, Jiyao Yang*, Hongjin Zhao, Xiaoyong Li, Yanzhe Ji, Xingjian Li, Runmin Jiang, Tianyang Wang, Saeed Anwar, Dongwoo Kim, Yue Yao, Zhenyue Qin, Min Xu

    * Equal contribution

    Introduces DermCase, a multimodal benchmark that evaluates diagnostic reasoning and differential diagnoses for rare skin diseases alongside final diagnostic accuracy.
